# Sunlit San Antonio Showcase

Build a premium, modern website for “Solar Vibes SA”, a professional solar panel cleaning & maintenance company in San Antonio, Texas.

Business info:

• Services: Solar Panel Cleaning, Maintenance, Animal/Bird Protection, Leak Inspections

• Service area: San Antonio & across Texas

• Phone: +1 210-891-9457

• Languages: English & Spanish

Design:

Create a high-end solar/energy-inspired website, NOT a generic template. Use a clean dark + warm solar aesthetic, premium typography, large spacing, subtle gradients, glass effects and smooth animations.

Sections:

1. Sticky premium navbar

2. Hero with a large solar-panel background video, strong headline, CTA buttons

3. Trust/statistics section

4. Services section with premium cards and images

5. Full-width video showcase section

6. “Why Solar Vibes SA” section with image + text

7. Before/After solar cleaning gallery using mixed images

8. Solar maintenance process section

9. Full-width visual/video section with overlay text

10. Customer reviews/testimonials

11. Final CTA with phone button

12. Premium footer

Use relevant solar panel, rooftop, cleaning, Texas and sunlight images/videos as visual placeholders. Mix videos and images so the page feels dynamic and never boring.

Video sections should autoplay, muted, loop and use lazy loading. Keep videos short and optimized for performance.

Make the website fully responsive, smooth-scrolling, fast-loading and visually impressive. Use subtle reveal animations, hover effects and polished transitions without over-animating.

Important: Keep the code clean and lightweight. Avoid unnecessary components, libraries and excessive animations. The final result should feel like an expensive professional agency website rather than a normal small-business website.
